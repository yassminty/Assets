gameplay rules:
collect the items around the house and outside the house to finish the game. you'll be rewarded with points and we would congratulate you after you finish the game! do enjoy playing the short game and oh, don't step into the pond.
 
gameplay controls:
W = Walk Forwards
A = Walk Left
S = Walk Backwards
D = Walk Right
Shift + WASD = Sprint/Run
Spacebar = Jump
E/LMB = Interact

Limitations:
controls might still be a little wonky. the door might over-open. score & health count might not work because there is might be something wrong with the script. the collectibles might not be able to be collected.
